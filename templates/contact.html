Our Mission

We believe in fostering a community where no one struggles to find blood in times of need. Our goal is to bridge the gap between blood donors and recipients by offering an easy-to-use platform that connects both parties.

How We Work

The BUP Blood Bank operates as a volunteer-driven effort. We collect a database of registered blood donors and allow those in need of blood to search for a match. We also ensure confidentiality and convenience by making the process quick and straightforward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is blood donation safe?

Yes, blood donation is completely safe. Trained professionals use sterile disposable equipment for each donor. The human body typically replaces the volume of donated blood within 24-48 hours.

How often can I donate blood?

Males can donate every 3 months (12 weeks) and females can donate every 4 months. Consult with medical professionals for your specific situation.

Can anyone become a blood donor?

While blood donation is crucial for saving lives, there are eligibility criteria. You must be at least 18 years old, weigh at least 45kg, and be in good health. Contact us for complete eligibility requirements.
