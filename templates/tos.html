Medical Disclaimer & Blood Donation Guidelines

Medical Disclaimer: BUP Blood Bank is not a medical service provider and does not perform medical examinations. All blood donation eligibility and safety decisions are the responsibility of medical professionals at designated blood banks or hospitals. We strongly recommend consulting healthcare providers before donating blood and following standard medical guidelines.

Important Medical Guidelines

  • Donors must be at least 18 years old and weigh at least 45kg
  • Donors must be in good health and not suffering from any illness
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding women should not donate blood
  • Individuals with recent tattoos, piercings, or medical procedures should wait appropriate periods
  • Those with chronic conditions should consult healthcare providers

Emergency Communications: In critical situations where immediate blood is required, users may contact suitable donors directly through provided contact information. However, BUP Blood Bank strongly recommends coordinating through medical facilities for proper blood banking protocols.
